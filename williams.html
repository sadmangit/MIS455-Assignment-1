Williams Racing

Williams was founded in 1977 by Frank Williams and Patrick Head and is based in Grove, Oxfordshire, England. The team started small, as a family-run independent team, but grew into one of the biggest names in F1 during the 1980s and 1990s. Its blue and white cars are a classic sight on the grid.

Williams has won 9 Constructors' Championships and 7 Drivers' Championships, which makes it one of the most successful teams ever. Its strongest years were between 1992 and 1997, when it won five constructors' titles thanks to advanced technology such as active suspension.

Legends and History

Great drivers such as Alan Jones, Keke Rosberg, Nelson Piquet, Nigel Mansell, Alain Prost, Damon Hill and Jacques Villeneuve all became World Champions with Williams. Mansell's 1992 title, driving the famous FW14B, is remembered as one of the most dominant seasons ever.

The team was bought by Dorilton Capital in 2020, and founder Frank Williams passed away in 2021. Today Williams is rebuilding and trying to move back up the grid, using Mercedes engines.
