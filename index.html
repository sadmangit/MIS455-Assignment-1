Scuderia Ferrari

Scuderia Ferrari is the oldest and most famous team in Formula 1. It was founded by Enzo Ferrari in 1929 as a racing team, and it has competed in every F1 World Championship season since the series began in 1950. The team is based in Maranello, Italy, and its bright red cars, painted in a shade called Rosso Corsa, are recognised by fans all over the world.

Ferrari's symbol is the prancing horse, and its passionate supporters are known as the Tifosi. When the team races at its home Grand Prix in Monza, the grandstands turn into a sea of red flags. With 16 Constructors' Championships, Ferrari has won more team titles than any other team in the sport.

Legends and History

Some of the greatest drivers in history have raced for Ferrari. Alberto Ascari won the team's first drivers' titles in 1952 and 1953, Niki Lauda won in 1975 and 1977, and Michael Schumacher led the team to five drivers' championships in a row from 2000 to 2004. Kimi Raikkonen was the last Ferrari driver to win the title, in 2007.

In 2025, seven-time World Champion Lewis Hamilton joined Ferrari to race alongside Charles Leclerc, one of the biggest driver moves in the history of the sport, though the team finished the season still chasing its next championship.
