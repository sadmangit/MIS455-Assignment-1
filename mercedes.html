Mercedes-AMG Petronas

The Mercedes name has a long history in racing. In the 1930s the German company's silver cars were nicknamed the Silver Arrows, a name the team still uses today. Mercedes returned to Formula 1 as a full works team in 2010 after buying the Brawn GP team, and it is based in Brackley, England.

After a few years of building up, Mercedes became the most dominant team of the modern hybrid era. It won eight Constructors' Championships in a row from 2014 to 2021, which is a record in Formula 1. The team is also famous for its silver and teal colours.

Legends and History

Lewis Hamilton won six of his seven World Championships with Mercedes (2014, 2015, 2017, 2018, 2019 and 2020), and Nico Rosberg won his only title with the team in 2016. Together they helped make Mercedes a symbol of precision engineering and teamwork.

Mercedes also builds its own power units (engines) and supplies them to other teams, such as McLaren and Williams, so its technology can be seen on the grid even in cars that are not silver.
