Red Bull Racing

Red Bull Racing was created in 2005 when the energy drink company Red Bull bought the Jaguar F1 team. The team is based in Milton Keynes, England, and is known for its bold, playful style and its clever aerodynamic designs. Its dark blue cars quickly went from being newcomers to title contenders.

Sebastian Vettel gave the team its first big success by winning four drivers' championships in a row from 2010 to 2013, and the team won four constructors' titles in the same years. Later, Max Verstappen became the team's star driver.

Legends and History

Max Verstappen became the youngest race winner in F1 history when he won the 2016 Spanish Grand Prix at the age of 18, and he won four drivers' championships in a row from 2021 to 2024. In 2023, Red Bull won 21 of the 22 races, one of the most dominant seasons ever.

Red Bull also runs a second team, now called Racing Bulls, which helps young drivers move up the ladder to F1. From 2026, the team has been building its own engines through Red Bull Powertrains, working together with Ford.
