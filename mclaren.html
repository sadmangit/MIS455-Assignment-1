McLaren

McLaren was founded in 1963 by Bruce McLaren, a racing driver from New Zealand. The team is based at the McLaren Technology Centre in Woking, England, and it is easy to spot on the track because of its bright papaya orange colour.

McLaren is one of the most successful teams in F1 history, with 10 Constructors' Championships, second only to Ferrari. The team won back-to-back titles in 2024 and 2025, ending a long wait since its previous title in 1998.

Legends and History

The 1980s and early 1990s were McLaren's golden era. Ayrton Senna and Alain Prost together won six drivers' titles for the team between 1985 and 1991. Mika Hakkinen won two titles in 1998 and 1999, and Lewis Hamilton won his first title with McLaren in 2008.

In 2025, Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri helped McLaren secure its 10th constructors' title at the Singapore Grand Prix, with six rounds still left on the calendar, while the two team-mates kept fighting each other for the drivers' championship.
